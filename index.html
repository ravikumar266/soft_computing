🐝 What is the Artificial Bee Colony (ABC) Algorithm? (Click to collapse / expand tutorial)

The Artificial Bee Colony (ABC) algorithm, created by Dervis Karaboga (2005), is a swarm-intelligence metaheuristic inspired by the intelligent nectar-foraging behavior of honeybees. A swarm of simulated bees searches a multidimensional continuous space to minimize an objective function \(f(\mathbf{x})\).

🐝 1. Employed Bees

Each employed bee is assigned to one food source \(\mathbf{x}_i\). It explores candidate solutions in its neighborhood using the candidate equation and performs greedy selection: if \(f(\mathbf{v}_i) < f(\mathbf{x}_i)\), it adopts the new position and resets trials to 0; otherwise, it keeps the old position and increments its trial counter.

👀 2. Onlooker Bees

Onlookers wait in the hive and observe the waggle dances of employed bees. They select food sources via roulette wheel selection proportional to nectar fitness \(P_i = \text{fit}_i / \sum \text{fit}_k\), giving promising sources more search effort and reinforcing quality discoveries.

🔍 3. Scout Bees & Limit

The Limit parameter controls exploitation vs exploration. When an employed food source fails to improve after trials > limit attempts, it is abandoned as exhausted. The bee becomes a scout and discovers a fresh uniform random position inside the bounds, escaping local traps.

Neighborhood Update Equation: v_{i,j} = x_{i,j} + φ_{i,j} × (x_{i,j} - x_{k,j}) where \(k \ne i\), \(j \in \{1, \dots, D\}\), and \(\phi \in [-1, 1]\). Greedy Fitness: fit = 1/(1+f) if \(f \ge 0\), else 1+|f|.

Each food source position xᵢ represents a candidate solution in the search space, and its nectar volume corresponds to the solution's fitness/cost value.
🐝 Employed Bees

Associated with specific food sources. They search neighbor positions using candidate generation and perform greedy selection.

👀 Onlooker Bees

Wait in the hive, watch dance routines, and probabilistically pick promising sources proportional to nectar quality (Roulette Wheel).

🧭 Scout Bees

When a food source cannot be improved after limit trials, it is abandoned. The bee becomes a scout and finds a fresh random source.
